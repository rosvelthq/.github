## Hi there 👋

Rosvelt is an AI development platform that turns product ideas, bugs, docs, and feature requests into live software. Instead of only organizing work, Rosvelt executes it through autonomous agents that plan, build, test, and ship.

## What we build

Rosvelt helps product and engineering teams move from backlog to production faster by combining:

- Product backlog management
- Autonomous software development agents
- Ticket-based feature execution
- Bug fixing and iteration workflows
- Product documentation and implementation context
- End-to-end delivery from idea to live software

## How we work

Our repositories support the Rosvelt platform, internal tools, integrations, infrastructure, SDKs, experiments, and product-facing systems.

Most repositories in this organization are private and proprietary. Public repositories, when available, are intended to support integrations, examples, SDKs, or developer resources.

## Contribution guidelines

Public contributions are only accepted in repositories that explicitly include contribution instructions.

Before contributing, please review:

- `CONTRIBUTING.md`
- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`
- `SECURITY.md`
- The repository-specific `LICENSE`

For private repositories, access and contributions are limited to authorized Rosvelt team members, contractors, and approved partners.
